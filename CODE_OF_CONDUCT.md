# Code of conduct

BYOK Chat is committed to a respectful, safe, and useful collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Protect credentials, private data, and unpublished security information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information or credentials;
- deliberately disrupting project infrastructure or community discussion;
- using security reports to pressure, extort, or publicly expose users; or
- sustained bad-faith participation after a maintainer asks for a behavior change.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, private project reports, and project-related public communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-confidential concern, contact the maintainers through an appropriate repository issue. For a confidential conduct concern, use the private maintainer-reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible, but information may be shared when required for safety or legal reasons.
